About Us

Are you tired of spending more time planning your vacation than actually enjoying it? We are Pedro, Andres, and Salvador, the founders of DestinAI. Our mission was born from a struggle we know well as immigrants in the United States: we travel often, but we kept getting lost in the planning, the logistics, and the search for experiences that actually fit us. DestinAI solves that by doing the research for you. It pulls real flight prices, hotel options, the weather forecast, and top-rated places matched to your interests, then uses Google Gemini to turn it all into a day-by-day itinerary built around your dates, your budget, and what you love. No endless tabs, no guesswork: just a plan that's personal, practical, and ready to go.

Step 1

Where & When?


First, tell us where your adventure begins and ends. We start by locking in the logistics: your chosen destination (like Miami), your departure city, the dates you're traveling, and your total trip budget in USD. This critical first step ensures that every recommendation we generate is instantly practical, meaning your finalized itinerary will be available for the right dates and perfectly aligned with your financial goals.

Step 2

Who Are You?

Next, we want to know a little about you and your crew! Input your age, gender, and the number of travelers in your group. This personal data is essential for tailoring your trip beyond the surface level. Knowing your age, for example, allows us to check local legal requirements for nightlife, while understanding your group size helps us suggest the best transportation and properly sized accommodations.

Step 3

What Do You Love?


Finally, tell us what sparks your joy by choosing as many options as you like from our detailed checklist of 38 interests! This is where the hyper-personalization truly shines. Whether you're passionate about Ancient Ruins, searching for the best Street Food, ready for Extreme Sports, or craving a Spa/Wellness Retreat, your selections inform every stop on the map.
